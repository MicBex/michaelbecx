YouTube censored my cash cow channel

I used to own a mid-size YouTube channel that got millions of views every month. Short-form vertical content at that point in time did not exist yet, so all of my views were long-form views. Now as you can imagine, this gave me the rare opportunity to make good money posting videos online.

After several years of growing this YouTube channel and raking in tens of thousands of dollars, I started receiving concerning notifications from Google: "Your content does not fit our platform". That was the beginning of the end for my cash cow channel.

The content that needed to be censored

When someone says their YouTube channel was deleted because of harmful and/or dangerous content, what would come to mind? For me, I'd imagine the glorification of terrorists & child predators, uploading tutorials on how to make DIY weapons or spreading fake news.

But I must disappoint you: I did not upload such content, yet did receive several take-down notifications from Google claiming my content fell under "harmful and/or dangerous content". The videos I'd upload were all about browsing the web for free. I would teach people to use ad-blockers, how to use free software & tools and even how to move from paid operating systems like Windows to free options like Linux.

I reached out to Google multiple times, asking them why my content was flagged in such a way because I knew that, if I were to get three of these "strikes" in a row, my channel would simply be deleted. No human ever responsed to me and every message I'd receive back, if I even got one back, was clearly written by a robot.

Three strikes

In a matter of days I started receiving demonetization notifications left and right. No video was safe and so my monthly earnings dropped tremendously. I still had my sponsors, but that was not enough to cover the bills.

I got the first two strikes rather quickly but remained hopeful because for a full month I didn't get a third strike. But then, when I was on vacation, I got the final notification I'd receive on my YouTube channel: "Your account has been suspended". The third strike had come through and so my channel was now done for and so was my dream of becoming a digital nomad.
