Work Experience

History Teacher

After receiving my Bachelor of Arts in History from Fontys (Tilburg, Netherlands) I started teaching bilingual history (Dutch & English) at several Dutch high schools on the VWO level. In the Netherlands I have taught a little over 6 years in total.

English Teacher

I would teach teenagers at several local high schools while teaching adults and the elderly in my spare time. I'd also occasionally teach elementary school students with disabilities as charity work.
